## Institutional Function

The Technological Committee represents limitations imposed by systems rather
than technology in general.

Its three established areas are:

- time
- resources
- connection

The committee is infrastructure. Its apparatuses are not characters, mascots,
or villains. They do not become angry, negotiate, or act maliciously. They
report the current state of the system and are technically correct.

Conceptual formulations such as "I've accounted for your time," "I've
accounted for your resources," and "I've accounted for your access" describe
the apparatus functions only. They are not dialogue, captions, or required
comic text.

## Punch Clock

Function:

Accounts for the authorized working interval.

Approved visual language:

- old industrial Mine Time / Shift Punch Clock
- analog clock face
- side lever
- punch-card or adding-machine slip
- steam whistle
- `SHIFT'S OVER`

The Punch Clock is ordinary workplace machinery with excessive authority. It
has no face, personality, or operator.

## System Terminal / Overmind

Function:

Reports system resources and availability.

Approved visual language:

- central institutional CRT and keyboard
- flanking reel-to-reel tape cabinets
- status lights, service panels, cooling vents, and heavy cable trunks
- surrounding machinery establishes that the visible terminal is only one
  interface to a much larger governing system

The apparent face on the CRT is interface pareidolia, not an intentional
avatar:

- two ordinary status blocks may accidentally read as eyes
- a horizontal cursor on a lower row may accidentally read as a flat mouth
- there is no nose, face outline, anatomy, emoticon, or deliberate expression
- perceived expression may change only when normal interface activity changes
  the status blocks or cursor
- the interface does not track or look at people

The status strip is variable comic language. Possible system-state messages
include:

- `MEMORY FULL`
- `CONTEXT FULL`
- `RESOURCE BUSY`
- `UNAVAILABLE`
- `PLEASE WAIT`
- `UPDATE REQUIRED`
- `TOO INTERESTING`

These are examples, not a requirement to display all messages or permanently
assign one message to the terminal.

## Connection Node / Wireless Router

Function:

Represents connection and access state.

Approved visual language:

- inherits the Wireless Router silhouette established in `Around Here's Most
  Wanted`
- low horizontal older-model router
- two antennas, with one plausibly bent
- restrained wear, scuffs, and age
- front status lights
- visible power cord and plug
- no words on or near the router

The communication state is the primary representative:

- a large wireless signal symbol floats above the smaller router
- the source dot and innermost arc remain strong
- larger outer arcs are broken, faint, or incomplete
- the router carries the signal state but is not anthropomorphized

## Visual Style

All three apparatuses use the established Around Here black-and-white
newspaper-comic language:

- organic hand-drawn contours
- restrained technical detail
- readable silhouettes
- no color
- no realistic rendering
- no sleek modern interface design
