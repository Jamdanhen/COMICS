## Origin

The Ham Sandwich originated as an actual discussion about an actual ham sandwich.

The original canonical sandwich is:

- Wonder Bread
- Chipped Ham
- Yellow Mustard
- American Cheese

Simple. Ordinary. Complete.

The discussion later evolved into improving the sandwich:

- Rye Bread
- Smoked Black Forest Ham
- Swiss Cheese
- Better Mustard

The improved sandwich is not a replacement. It is an evolution. The original remains the canonical reference.

## Core Meaning

The Ham Sandwich represents the tendency to overcomplicate things when the correct answer already exists in a simple, functional form.

The lesson is not "Never improve." The lesson is "Do not mistake complexity for quality."

Strong fundamentals come first. Refinement comes second.

## Workshop Function

Within Around Here, the Ham Sandwich serves as a constant reminder to return to fundamentals.

When discussions become increasingly elaborate, the Ham Sandwich asks:

- What was the original problem?
- What was the simple solution?

## Visual Language

The Ham Sandwich should always appear as the original sandwich:

- Wonder Bread
- Chipped Ham
- Yellow Mustard
- American Cheese

No gourmet presentation. No visual emphasis. Its ordinary appearance is essential to the symbol.

## The Vending Machine

The workshop vending machine always contains the Ham Sandwich.

No one is certain how long it has been there. It is never removed. It is always available.

The sandwich exists as a reminder that simple, well-understood solutions remain available even while increasingly complicated ideas are being discussed.

## Continuity

The Ham Sandwich is a recurring continuity artifact. Like the Horn of Eld, it survives endings and reappearances.

When the workshop changes, projects evolve, or the mythology expands, the Ham Sandwich is still in the vending machine.

## Common Use

- "Maybe this is a Ham Sandwich."
- "We're making this harder than it needs to be."
- "Check the vending machine."

## Final Observation

The Ham Sandwich is not important because it is special. It is important because it remains ordinary.

Around Here may continually evolve. The Ham Sandwich reminds everyone where the foundation began.
