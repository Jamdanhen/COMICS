## Standing Representatives

The current Biological Committee delegation consists of:

- Mother Nature
- the Sandman
- the cafeteria lady

The old-fashioned family doctor is a consultant to the committee, not a
standing representative.

## Representative Functions

Mother Nature represents broad biological authority and the full range of
natural imperatives. Her visual language includes restrained wind, stirring
leaves, and water in motion. She is composed, authoritative, and difficult to
overrule.

The Sandman represents sleep, fatigue, and involuntary shutdown. He wears an
old-fashioned nightshirt and stocking cap, carries a tied sandbag, and lets
sleep sand fall from one hand. He is weary and intelligent, not foolish,
sinister, or childlike.

The cafeteria lady represents hunger, hydration, nourishment, and immediate
physical maintenance. She carries an institutional lunch tray and serving
spoon. She is practical and approachable rather than angry.

The family doctor provides medical interpretation when illness, injury, or
measurable physical limits become relevant. Medical interruption has not been
a recurring pressure on the work, so he remains a consultant.

## Visual Language

The representatives use the established Around Here black-and-white
newspaper-comic style:

- organic hand-drawn contours
- economical cartoon faces
- readable recurring silhouettes
- restrained detail
- no color
- no realistic rendering

The delegation deliberately combines mythic personifications with ordinary
institutional authority. Mother Nature and the Sandman embody biological
forces; the cafeteria lady grounds those forces in everyday maintenance.

## Acting Direction

The Biological Committee issues legitimate warnings that Interestingness
repeatedly delays or defeats.

Mother Nature may show controlled disapproval.

The Sandman may appear tired, patient, or restrainedly annoyed.

The cafeteria lady should remain practical and generally approachable, not
habitually angry.

The doctor should appear intelligent, experienced, and professionally
concerned.
