# Concept - The Bench Outside The Mine: Accumulated Traces

Status: working comic concept only / no comic draft generated

Date recorded: 2026-08-10

## Established Basis

The Bench outside the Mine is an established Around Here location where:

- stories are exchanged
- symbols connect
- humor becomes insight
- ore becomes usable

Source authority:

`C:\COMICS\Source\References\The_Mine_Foundational_Symbol_Case_Study.md`

## Current Concept

Begin with the empty workbench outside the Mine.

Across a sequence of visual beats, representatives or traces from different
projects pass the bench and leave one small object behind. The visitors do not
need to remain. Their accumulated objects become the material waiting for the
next conversation.

Possible deposits include:

- Zodak or a Masters of the Universe trace leaving Zodak's Helmet
- a role-playing or campaign trace leaving a die
- the living Idea Blob rolling past and leaving a small Idea Spud

The exact campaign die remains unresolved. A twenty-sided die reads broadly as
tabletop role-playing and Dungeons & Dragons. A six-sided die would be more
specific to Shadowrun. The eventual choice should follow the project being
represented rather than treating the systems as interchangeable.

## Possible Final Beat

Taskmaster and Razorforge are now seated at the bench and deeply engaged in a
conversation generated by the accumulated objects.

One of them may be holding or examining one of the deposited objects while the
other responds through attentive, animated posture. Dialogue is not yet
defined and may not be necessary.

Their lunches sit untouched:

- brown paper lunch bags
- an ordinary visible Ham Sandwich
- possibly another simple lunch item belonging to Razorforge

The Ham Sandwich should remain the canonical plain version:

- Wonder Bread
- chipped ham
- yellow mustard
- American cheese

It should not receive gourmet presentation or visual emphasis.

## Comic Engine

The intended progression is:

1. Empty place for a break.
2. Separate projects leave small physical traces.
3. The traces accumulate into relationships.
4. Taskmaster and Razorforge spend the break discussing those relationships
   while the actual lunch remains untouched.

The humor is not simply that they forgot to eat. The designated pause becomes
another site of discovery because the collected material is too interesting to
ignore.

This can show Interestingness indirectly through its effects without giving it
a mascot or fixed symbol.

## Current Strengths

- Establishes the Bench through action rather than explanation.
- Connects multiple projects without requiring labels or exposition.
- Gives approved symbols a natural recurring environment.
- Makes conversation visually consequential.
- Uses the untouched lunch as a quiet final joke.
- Can establish a repeatable setting for later conversational comics.
