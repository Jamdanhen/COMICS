# Committee Representative Portfolio Plan

Status: working concept / representative roster planning

Date: 2026-07-20

## Purpose

Develop portfolio sheets for the committees and their recurring representatives in Around Here.

The portfolio sheets are character and visual-language references, not comic pages. A committee may be represented by two or three recurring parties, but the representatives should remain distinct from the committee's tools, notices, and jurisdictional objects.

## Shared Setting Concept

Committees may eventually appear together in an Around Here town-hall chamber.

The chamber should suggest a civic assembly or old municipal hall rather than a futuristic senate:

- raised central speaking area
- curved or semicircular seating
- wooden desks and nameplates
- ordinary lamps, railings, papers, and microphones only where useful
- institutional seriousness applied to unusual pressures
- no sleek science-fiction architecture

This is a setting direction, not yet an approved comic scene.

### Technological Committee

Status: approved committee and approved apparatus portfolio.

Jurisdiction:

- device limits
- usage limits
- tokens
- context windows
- synchronization failures
- interval expiration

Established visual language:

- Mine Time Clock
- steam whistle
- adding-machine slip
- `INTERVAL EXPIRED`
- `TOKEN LIMIT EXCEEDED`

Current apparatus direction:

- Punch Clock: accounts for authorized time
- System Terminal / Overmind: accounts for available system resources
- Connection Node / Wireless Router: accounts for access

The explanatory formulations are conceptual only. They are not dialogue,
captions, or required machine text.

The apparatuses are not malicious. They do not negotiate, become angry, or act
as mascots. They report the system's current state and are technically correct.

System Terminal direction:

- visible terminal is only an interface to a larger governing system
- the apparent face is interface pareidolia rather than an intentional avatar
- two ordinary status blocks may accidentally read as eyes
- a horizontal cursor on a lower row may accidentally read as a flat mouth
- there is no nose, facial anatomy, face outline, emoticon, or deliberate
  expression
- perceived expression may change only because the status blocks or cursor
  change during normal interface activity
- the interface does not track or look at people
- surrounding reel-to-reel banks, status lights, service panels, and cable
  trunks imply a much larger system
- the status strip is variable comic language rather than permanent wording
- concise system-state messages may include `MEMORY FULL`, `CONTEXT FULL`,
  `RESOURCE BUSY`, `UNAVAILABLE`, `PLEASE WAIT`, `UPDATE REQUIRED`, or
  `TOO INTERESTING`

Connection Node direction:

- inherits the approved Wireless Router silhouette from `Around Here's Most
  Wanted`
- low horizontal router body, two antennas, front status lights, and visible
  power cord
- one antenna may be bent and the older router may show restrained wear
- the router remains small while a large distressed low-signal wireless icon
  becomes the primary visual representative
- only the source dot and innermost arc remain strong; larger arcs appear
  broken, faint, or incomplete
- no words on or near the Connection Node

### Boundary Commission

Status: approved infrastructure authority; representatives not yet defined.

Jurisdiction:

- what belongs Around Here
- recurrence
- jurisdictional limits
- preventing random material from becoming canon

Established visual language:

- caution tape
- `RECURRENCE DO NOT CROSS`

Current candidate representatives:

- Beaver civil engineer / senior infrastructure specialist
- jurisdictional official / referee

The beaver is a working direction, not yet an approved character. The role is
earned through natural behavior: building, surveying, redirecting water,
maintaining infrastructure, and establishing boundaries. He is not a mascot.

Character details:

- carpenter's pencil with diagonal beaver tooth marks
- pencil visibly chewed into a surprisingly functional sharpened point; this is required
- coffee mug overflowing with half-chewed carpenter pencils
- immaculate blueprints despite the pencil problem

These are tiny visual truths to be noticed rather than explained.

The referee represents judgment at the line rather than sport. His current
candidate language includes:

- whistle
- folded penalty flag
- free hands and a mobile officiating stance
- attentive focus on the action requiring a ruling

The referee does not carry a clipboard and does not monitor his own position
against the boundary. Referees move through and across the field while watching
the event they have authority to judge.

Candidate enforcement instrument:

- unpersonified folding municipal barricade
- established `RECURRENCE DO NOT CROSS` tape physically draped over it

The barricade is equipment, not a representative or mascot.

### Classification Board / Category Authority

Status: implied authority; not yet separately characterized.

Jurisdiction:

- category errors
- candidate versus canon
- draft versus final
- source versus derivation
- labels that do not fit

The Chief Inspector does not belong to this committee or board. He is an
independent investigator with a specific jurisdiction: category fraud. He may
investigate an object or label that comes before the Classification Authority,
but he is not one of its representatives.

Representative roster: open.

### Chief Inspector / Category Fraud Division

Status: approved independent character and investigative office.

Jurisdiction:

- category fraud
- false classification
- mislabeling
- identity drift
- unauthorized symbolic promotion

He may enter a committee matter when category fraud is suspected, but he does
not sit on the committee and should not be used as its representative.

## Registered Conceptual Committees

These are recorded in the committee register but do not yet need individual portfolio sheets:

- Committee of Previously Answered Questions
- Committee of Token Conservation
- Committee of Mechanical Barricades
- Committee of Excessive Refinement
- Committee of Shiny New Projects
- Committee of Unresolved Noodles
- Committee of Campaign Continuity

Their current visual forms are better treated as objects, pressures, or notices until a recurring representative becomes necessary.

Discovery is not a committee. It is workshop activity carried out by the core
participants as they explore, excavate, notice, and investigate.

The Committee of Done is removed from the project vocabulary. It had no
precedent, history, roots, recurring language, or established symbol.

## Current Decision Boundary

The Comedy Standards and Biological Committee delegations are approved. The
Biological Committee has three standing representatives plus a family-doctor
consultant. Technological and Work Governance representatives remain open and
should not be silently invented as approved characters.

The Boundary Commission now has a strong proposed representative direction: a
beaver functioning as a civil engineer. This should guide future portfolio
development without promoting the character automatically.
